Partner Health & Churn
Early Warning System

An agentic system that watches partner supply, utilization, and payout signals across 12,000+ gyms, studios, and sports facilities in 150 cities, and flags at-risk partners to city heads before they go silent.

Today: FITPASS is reactive. A partner either reaches out when something's wrong, or they quietly stop listing slots and vanish with no intervention.

This system: continuously scores every partner, tiers them A through E by value and demand context, and routes the ones losing health to the right city head before churn happens.
