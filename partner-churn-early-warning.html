FITPASS · Agentic AI Prototype

Partner Churn - Early Warning System

FITPASS today only learns a partner is unhappy when they reach out, or when they've already gone silent. This project designs an agentic system that watches supply, demand, and payout signals across 12,000+ gym, studio, and sports facility partners in 150 cities, and flags who's at risk before they leave.

FITPASS AI/ML Retention Agentic Systems Internal Tools

The problem

FITPASS's partner network is the supply side of the marketplace. When a gym or studio quietly reduces the slots it lists, or stops getting bookings, that's an early signal of disengagement, long before the partner is picked up by an SLA or a complaint. Today, that signal goes unwatched. Partners either escalate themselves, or they churn silently, and the org finds out after the fact.

The goal of this system is to move FITPASS from reactive to proactive: continuously score every partner's health, tier them by commercial value and market context, and route the ones losing health to the right city head before churn happens, not after.

How it works

Sensing

The agent monitors four signals per partner, refreshed monthly:

SignalWhat it tells us
Slots listedSupply the partner is choosing to offer, often the first sign of pulling back
Slots bookedUtilization, demand actually meeting that supply
Attendance confirmedReal usage versus a booking that never converts
Payout per cycleThe commercial reason a partner has to stay

Reasoning

Detection isn't a single threshold. It's a two-step check: a partner is provisionally flagged against their own trailing 3-month baseline, then verified against city-wide demand context before a human ever sees it, so a citywide soft month doesn't get mistaken for one partner's problem.

Tiering

Every partner is scored A through E using payout per slot and city demand density together, since a premium partner in a quiet market carries different risk than the same partner in a saturated one.

Action & routing

Flagged partners route to the relevant city head: Tier A and B inline in a monthly email with contact details and a suggested next step, Tier C through E one click away on an internal dashboard. Claiming a partner and logging outreach syncs status to CRM automatically, so managers get visibility without a separate report.

Deliberate scope decisions, not gaps: detection is rule-based against trailing baselines, not an ML model, this keeps the system explainable to a non-technical stakeholder and buildable without a data science dependency. The "before and after" review each cycle is a human-reviewed comparison, not adaptive retraining. Tier payout thresholds are flagged as an open input requiring real FITPASS data, not fabricated for this mock.
